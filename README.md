One image that sums up Saturday 21 November 2026: 43 stories, 7 regions, 13 illustrated cards. This revision of the red-band poster (v10) draws a real world map from Natural Earth 110m coastline data, colours the list numbers 14-43 by region — each with a matching coloured pin on the map — and gives the three hero pictures richer, more realistic scenes: the COP31 summit hall, Riyadh's night skyline under interception, and the Andean quake.
